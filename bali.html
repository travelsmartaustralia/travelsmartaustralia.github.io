Indonesia • Island escape & culture

Bali holiday guide and budget

Bali can suit beach breaks, food-focused trips, cultural visits and nature escapes. The best base depends on what you want from the holiday and how much time you want to spend travelling between areas.

Travel Smart Australia • Destination planning guide
Planning note: travel rules, transport schedules, prices and opening hours can change. Verify important details with official sources before making arrangements.

Choose the right base

Different parts of the island have different atmospheres. Research beach areas, inland towns and quieter regions before choosing accommodation. Consider traffic and transfer times.

Plan transport realistically

Road travel can take longer than distances suggest. Allow extra time for transfers, especially around busy periods, and use reputable transport providers.

Balance activities and downtime

Avoid filling every day with long drives. Group nearby sights together and leave time to enjoy your accommodation and local area.

Respect local customs

Dress appropriately when visiting temples, follow posted guidance and be respectful during ceremonies. Check local rules before visiting sacred sites.

Choose accommodation carefully

Look at location, recent reviews, included facilities, cancellation rules and the full cost including taxes or fees.

Before you go

  • Check passport validity and entry requirements with the relevant official government authority.
  • Arrange suitable travel insurance and understand what it covers.
  • Check transport schedules, baggage rules and accommodation cancellation conditions.
  • Save important addresses and emergency contact details offline.

Australia-focused planning guide

How much does a Bali holiday cost from Australia?

Bali can work for a short beach escape or a longer island holiday. For a first visit, pick one or two bases rather than changing hotels every night.

Five-day activity ideas

Spend a day settling in at the beach, a day exploring Ubud and nearby scenery, a day visiting a coastal viewpoint, a day enjoying local cafés or a cooking class, and a flexible final day for shopping and relaxation. Check travel times before combining distant attractions.

Practical checks

Before departure, verify Indonesian entry and tourist levy rules, passport validity, travel insurance, and local transport arrangements using official sources. For more planning help, use our hotel selection guide, travel tools and trip planner.
