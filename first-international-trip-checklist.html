First international trip: practical checklist

Preparing a few essentials early can make an international trip much smoother.

Before you book

Check passport validity, entry rules for your nationality, travel advisories and any transit requirements. Requirements can change.

After booking

Save copies of confirmations, review baggage limits and consider suitable travel insurance. Check medical and activity exclusions carefully.

One week before departure

Arrange mobile connectivity, check airport transfer options, confirm accommodation arrival details and download offline maps.

On travel day

Keep your passport, essential medication, valuables and a change of clothes in carry-on luggage where permitted.

For Australian travellers, consult Smartraveller and official destination government sources.
