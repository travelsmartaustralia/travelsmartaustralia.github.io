Protect your trip

Travel insurance: what to compare

Travel insurance policies differ. Choose cover based on your destination, activities, health needs, trip cost and personal circumstances. Read the Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) before buying.

🩺

Medical and emergency cover

Check overseas medical expenses, hospital care, emergency evacuation and repatriation limits. Review exclusions and any pre-existing medical condition rules.

🧳

Cancellation and belongings

Compare cancellation reasons, limits, excesses, baggage cover, valuables sub-limits and documentation requirements.

🏄

Activities and exclusions

Confirm whether activities such as scooter riding, diving, hiking or snow sports are covered and what licence or safety conditions apply.

Australian travel advice: Read the Australian Government's guidance on travel insurance before choosing a policy. Insurance is not a substitute for checking official travel advice.

Before installing an eSIM

  • Confirm your phone supports eSIM and is not carrier-locked.
  • Check the plan covers every country on your itinerary.
  • Understand when validity begins: purchase, installation or first connection.
  • Keep your primary number active only if you understand roaming charges.
  • Install over reliable Wi-Fi and keep setup instructions available offline.
