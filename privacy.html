Privacy policy

How this basic informational website handles information.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Travel Smart Australia aims to respect visitors' privacy. This website is a static information website and does not currently provide user accounts, payment processing, newsletter subscriptions or booking services.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and enquiry details. We use it to respond and manage the enquiry.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies in its current starter configuration. Some externally hosted images may be requested from third-party image hosts, which may receive standard technical information such as your IP address. Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

Affiliate links

If affiliate links are added in the future, clicking them may involve third-party tracking technologies and privacy practices. We will update this policy to describe relevant services before using them.

Contact

For privacy questions, email travelsmartaustralia@gmail.com.

This is a starter privacy notice, not legal advice. Review and update it if you add analytics, advertising, cookies, embedded maps, forms, mailing lists or affiliate tracking.
