Southeast Asia • Food, culture & coastline

Vietnam travel guide and 6-day itinerary

Vietnam offers a varied mix of energetic cities, historic towns, food experiences and coastal scenery. A first visit is easier when you choose a manageable route rather than trying to see the whole country at once.

Travel Smart Australia • Destination planning guide
Planning note: travel rules, transport schedules, prices and opening hours can change. Verify important details with official sources before making arrangements.

Choose a route that suits your time

For a shorter holiday, focus on one region or connect two destinations with sensible travel time. Longer journeys can include northern, central and southern highlights, but allow for transfers and rest days.

Think about the season

Weather differs across the country and can vary by region. Check forecasts and seasonal conditions for each stop rather than assuming one season applies everywhere.

Getting around

Compare domestic flights, trains, buses and private transfers based on journey time, arrival location, luggage and comfort. Confirm schedules directly with operators before travel.

Food and local experiences

Explore local markets, regional dishes and guided experiences where useful. Be considerate of local customs and check food ingredients if you have allergies.

Where to stay

Choose accommodation based on the neighbourhood, transport access, recent guest feedback, total price and cancellation terms. A central location may save time, but compare the trade-offs.

Before you go

  • Check passport validity and entry requirements with the relevant official government authority.
  • Arrange suitable travel insurance and understand what it covers.
  • Check transport schedules, baggage rules and accommodation cancellation conditions.
  • Save important addresses and emergency contact details offline.

Vietnam itinerary from Perth: Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang and Hoi An

This fast-paced itinerary works best for travellers who want a taste of southern and central Vietnam. It requires a domestic flight each way and leaves little room for delays. For a slower holiday, add two or three nights.

Where to stay

Choose District 1 in Ho Chi Minh City for a short first visit, the My Khe Beach area for Da Nang beach access, and a walkable location near Hoi An Ancient Town for evening photography. Check recent reviews, cancellation conditions and transfer times before paying.
