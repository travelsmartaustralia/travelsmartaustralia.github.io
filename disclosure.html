Affiliate disclosure

Our approach to recommendations, partnerships and possible commissions.

Current status

Travel Smart Australia is not currently claiming to be an approved Booking.com affiliate or partner. We do not currently earn commissions from Booking.com bookings through this website.

Future affiliate links

If we join an affiliate programme, some links may be tracked links. If a reader clicks one and completes a qualifying action, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Any such links will be clearly identified.

Editorial approach

Commercial relationships will not change our commitment to providing useful, transparent travel information. Readers should compare accommodation details, total prices, guest reviews, location and cancellation conditions before booking.
