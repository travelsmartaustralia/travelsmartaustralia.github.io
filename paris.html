France • Europe • City break

Paris city guide

Paris rewards slow exploration. Beyond famous landmarks, its neighbourhoods, markets, galleries and cafés make it worth planning time between major attractions.

Travel Smart Australia • Destination planning guide
Planning note: travel rules, transport schedules, prices and opening hours can change. Verify important details with official sources before making arrangements.

Plan by neighbourhood

Group nearby attractions together to reduce backtracking. Leave space for unplanned walks and breaks.

Use public transport

Review official metro, bus and airport transfer information before arrival. Check ticket types and current service updates through official transport sources.

Book popular attractions thoughtfully

Some museums and landmarks use timed entry or advance reservations. Check official attraction websites for current opening times and ticket conditions.

Be aware of your belongings

As in any busy city, keep valuables secure in crowded transport hubs and tourist areas.

Pick a practical location

Compare accommodation access to public transport, likely daily routes, guest reviews and cancellation terms.

Before you go

  • Check passport validity and entry requirements with the relevant official government authority.
  • Arrange suitable travel insurance and understand what it covers.
  • Check transport schedules, baggage rules and accommodation cancellation conditions.
  • Save important addresses and emergency contact details offline.
