Japan • Destination guide

Tokyo travel guide

Food markets, neighbourhoods and day trips. Practical ideas for planning your first visit.

Travel Smart Australia • Independent travel guide
Travel requirements, prices, weather and opening times change. Confirm important details with official sources before booking.

What to see and do

Explore Asakusa and Shibuya, plan a day for museums or gardens, and allow time for neighbourhood food spots.

Getting around

Rail connections are extensive; check station exits and luggage rules before busy transfers.

Where to stay

Choose accommodation based on the places you want to visit, transport access, recent independent reviews and the full cost. Check cancellation terms and any additional charges.

Suggested three-day outline

  • Day 1: Arrive, settle into your accommodation and explore the nearby neighbourhood.
  • Day 2: Visit the main attractions that interest you, grouping nearby stops together.
  • Day 3: Explore a different area, enjoy local food and leave time for an unhurried departure.

Before you travel

  • Confirm passport, visa and entry requirements using official government sources.
  • Compare flights, accommodation locations and transport options.
  • Consider suitable travel insurance and check exclusions.
  • Check seasonal weather and local customs.
