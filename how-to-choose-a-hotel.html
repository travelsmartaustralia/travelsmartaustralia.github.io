How to choose a hotel without surprises

Hotel photos can be appealing, but the best choice usually comes down to location, total cost and booking conditions. Use this checklist before reserving a room.

1. Compare the full price

Check taxes, local fees, breakfast, parking and any charges collected at the property. Compare prices for the same room type and cancellation policy.

2. Check the neighbourhood

Look at travel time to the sights you actually plan to visit. A cheaper hotel can cost more overall if transport is inconvenient.

3. Read recent reviews thoughtfully

Look for patterns in comments about cleanliness, noise, air conditioning and service. One unusual review may not reflect the typical stay.

4. Understand the cancellation rules

Check the exact deadline, local time zone and whether payment is taken immediately or later.

5. Confirm what is included

Verify bed configuration, Wi-Fi, airport transfers and accessibility needs directly with the property when important.
